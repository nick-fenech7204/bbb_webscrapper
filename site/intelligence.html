What the columns mean
  • BBB grade / BBB complaints — the letter grade and the count of complaints filed with BBB (complaints show only for datasets scraped with full detail).
  • Yelp ★ / Yelp # — the matched Yelp listing's star rating and review count (rating links to Yelp).
  • Reputation (0–100) — blended "how weak is this business's public reputation": BBB grade, BBB review average + complaints, and Yelp rating + volume, weighted over whatever's available. Higher = weaker.
  • Lead priority (0–130) — how good a sales lead this is for a firm that sells review / reputation-management services. It favors the salvageable middle — a visible, fixable problem at a business established enough to pay — so both the already-fine and the beyond-help score lower. Bumps for a reputation gap, an accredited business, an active (but not runaway) complaint history, and 10+ years in business.
  • Flags — Reputation gap: BBB grade looks clean (A− or better) but the actual feedback doesn't. Accredited, low-rated. Few reviews: under 25 Yelp reviews.

Derived scoring is a v1 starting point, not a finished model.
