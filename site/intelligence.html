What the columns mean
  • Yelp — the matched Yelp listing's star rating and review count (links to Yelp).
  • BBB−Yelp gap — BBB letter grade and Yelp stars both put on a 0–5 scale, BBB minus Yelp. Positive = BBB looks kinder than Yelp.
  • Review-need (0–100) — how low the Yelp rating is (60%) plus how thin the review volume is (40%). Higher = more likely to want review help.
  • Lead priority — Review-need plus bumps for reputation divergence, an accredited-but-low-rated business, and an established (10+ yr) business.
  • Flags — BBB↑ Yelp↓: BBB grade A− or better but a reviewed Yelp rating under 3. Accredited, low-rated. Few reviews: under 25 Yelp reviews.

Derived scoring is a v1 starting point, not a finished model.
