What the columns mean
  • BBB — the letter grade BBB gives the business, with a checkmark if they're BBB-accredited.
  • BBB complaints — the number of complaints filed with BBB (appears only for datasets scraped with full detail).
  • Yelp — the matched Yelp listing's star rating and review count (links to Yelp).
  • Reputation (0–100) — blended “how weak is this business's public reputation”: BBB grade, BBB review average + complaints, and Yelp rating + volume, weighted over whatever's available. Higher = weaker.
  • Lead priority (0–130) — how good a sales lead this is for a firm that sells review / reputation-management services. It favors the salvageable middle — a visible, fixable problem at a business established enough to pay — so both the already-fine and the beyond-help score lower. A missing phone number pulls this down too: the best-fit lead is dead weight this week with no way to call it.
  • Reach — is there enough here to actually contact this business today: a phone number, and ideally a named contact (BBB's listed principal/manager) so the call lands on a real person instead of a front desk. Hover a badge for the full detail.
  • Flags — Reputation gap: BBB grade looks clean (A− or better) but the actual feedback doesn't. Accredited, low-rated. Few reviews: under 25 Yelp reviews.

Scoring is refined continuously as more market data comes in.
